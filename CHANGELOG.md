## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- The Bank of No in cross-section: boxes on three floors (rules of the day, laws, core beliefs), with red string between what rests on what.
- 14 security systems, one per thinking trap, each taken down by three hits.
- The crew: evidence (the Pick), Socratic questions (the Professor, 304), the guard's duty log (the Shadow), the appeal and the engraving (the Quill), behavioural experiments (the Driver, 224 ready jobs).
- 192 ready boxes in 8 spheres, or your own; going down to find what a belief rests on.
- Cracking the safe, the velvet case of loot, the guard coming back.
- The Shadow's evening round, a help page for crisis words, English and Russian with Russian gender forms.
